**Name**
> MarketplaceOnly

**Tagline** (60 max)
> Facebook, just Marketplace. No feed, no Reels.

**Description** (260 max)
> A free Chrome and Firefox extension for people who only use Facebook to buy and sell. It lands you on Marketplace and hides the feed, Reels, Groups, Gaming, and notifications. Messenger always works. No data collected.

**Maker's first comment**
> Hi Product Hunt! I only open Facebook for Marketplace, but every visit starts in the feed. MarketplaceOnly makes Facebook behave like a standalone Marketplace app: it redirects everything else to Marketplace, hides the distracting icons, and gives you a small tray to switch anything back on.
>
> It's built around "fail open": if Facebook redesigns something, the worst case is extra clutter, never a broken Marketplace. And it's private by design: no accounts, no analytics, no server.
>
> I'm a product leader and built it with Claude Code from a written spec; the spec and code are open source. I'd love feedback, especially anything it misses on your Facebook.
